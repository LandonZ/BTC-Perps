Strategy and assumptions

Multiple pairs can remain open. Spot prices use the latest trade as a midpoint proxy with an assumed spread. Queue priority, depth, impact, borrow costs, margin and liquidation are not modeled. The chart uses sampled minute observations; maximum drawdown is calculated from every replay event.
